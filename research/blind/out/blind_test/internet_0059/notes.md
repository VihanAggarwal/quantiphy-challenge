# internet_0059 - basketball tossed in front of wall with 3 framed posters (handheld camera)
Method: ball tracked by red colour mask (frames 66-97, full-res centres); camera shake removed by phase correlation
on poster region vs frame 78 (shift up to ~15 px). Parabola fit of compensated y: a = 4970 px/s^2 (sub-ranges 4800-5235)
-> scale 0.001972 m/px (g = 9.8). Ball and wall assumed same scale (ball diameter 22.9 cm = basketball-plausible).
Ball diameter: minor-axis extent of mask ~116 px. Poster outer edges from intensity profiles at frame 78:
left 436-702 x 276-667 (h 391 px); middle 744-1124 (w 380 px); right 1159-1409 x ~495-667 (h 172 px); L-left to R-right 973 px.
Velocities: speed from local quadratic fits (+-3 frames) at frames 75/90 (backward diff 3.24 / 4.26); displacement |p90-p75|;
avg velocity = |p96-p75|/0.7 s.
Confidence: scale +-5% (acceleration spread), sizes medium, motion medium-high.
